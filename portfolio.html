1 / COURSE DEVELOPMENT

Meta Quest 3 Teachers Guide

August 2026–Present

VIEW COMPLETE PROJECT ↗VIEW SCREENSHOT ↗

Introduction

I began developing the Meta Quest 3 Teachers Guide in August 2026 and continue to refine it. This web-based resource introduces teachers and other first-time users to the headset, its controls, basic navigation, and learning activities. It combines visual guidance with practical setup instructions to make an unfamiliar device easier to explore. The intended audience is educators preparing to use virtual reality in teaching. Audience feedback has not yet been documented in this portfolio.

Significance

This artifact shows how I translate unfamiliar technology into a manageable learning experience. The guide connects visual explanations with practical tasks so teachers can move from identifying controls to using the headset. It reflects my focus on clear instructions, purposeful technology use, and helping learners build confidence before entering a new environment.

2 / PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT

International Online Education Website

September 2026–Present

VIEW COMPLETE PROJECT ↗VIEW SCREENSHOT ↗

Introduction

I began developing the International Online Education Website in September 2026 and continue to update it. This bilingual website helps students and families explore language learning, academic support, study abroad planning, visa services, and arrival support. Color-coded service cards and consistent navigation organize these options into a clear starting point. The intended audience is people seeking information and support for international education. Audience feedback has not yet been documented in this portfolio.

Significance

This artifact demonstrates my ability to organize a broad range of education services into a coherent digital experience. Bilingual labels, consistent navigation, and distinct service cards make the information easier to scan. The project connects my background in international education with my interest in designing useful, approachable tools for students and families.

3 / IMMERSIVE LEARNING DESIGN

Teotihuacan VR Museum

September 2026–Present

EDUC 767 · Group project with Ava and Regan

VIEW COMPLETE PROJECT ↗VIEW SCREENSHOT ↗

Introduction

I began developing Teotihuacan VR Museum with Ava and Regan in September 2026 as a group assignment for EDUC 767. Built in Frame, this virtual museum explores how an immersive environment can make abstract knowledge more concrete and memorable. Our aim was to experiment with spatial layout, directional sound, controller vibration or interaction, and stereoscopic vision as possible supports for learning and memory. The intended audience is learners exploring Teotihuacan through a virtual museum experience. The project remains in development, and audience feedback has not yet been documented in this portfolio.

Significance

This project helped me think about learning design beyond a screen or a page. Working with Ava and Regan gave me an opportunity to connect educational ideas with the design of a shared virtual space. Its significance lies in exploring how sensory cues and purposeful interaction might support understanding and recall. These are design intentions rather than verified learning outcomes.
