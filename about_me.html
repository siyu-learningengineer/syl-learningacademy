About Me

Professional Background

I have more than ten years of experience in international education. I have worked as a Director of College Counseling, Principal’s Assistant, Director of Studies, and language teacher. In these roles, I designed courses and learning materials, trained teachers, evaluated teaching, and managed educational programs. At UNC-Chapel Hill and through my internship with the UNC School of Social Work, I am building skills in Canvas course development, digital accessibility, generative AI, and technology-supported learning.

Career Aspirations & Goals

I want to work as an instructional designer who creates accessible, engaging learning experiences. I bring classroom experience and academic leadership to this work. I want to help schools, universities, and other organizations connect learning goals with practical activities and useful technology. Over time, I hope to lead learning programs and use AI responsibly to support more inclusive, personalized learning.

Learning Design Interests

I am interested in how technology and AI can help students reflect on their learning and make informed decisions. My interests include supporting students in Grades 9–12 as they understand their strengths, prepare for college, and plan their next steps. I believe technology should help students become better learners. This means making instructions clear and tasks manageable. For example, my Meta Quest 3 guide uses a labeled image to help new users identify headset controls before setting up the device and opening a learning activity.
