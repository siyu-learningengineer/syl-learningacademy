Hello, I'm Siyu Liu

Learning Engineer | Instructional Designer | Educational Technology

I am an educator and learning design professional with more than a decade of experience across international K–12 education, college counseling, curriculum and instructional development, teacher development, and educational program leadership. I am currently pursuing a Master of Arts in Educational Innovation, Technology, and Entrepreneurship (MEITE) at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, building on my previous M.A. in Education from Arizona State University. Through MEITE, with a focus on Learning Engineering, I am developing expertise for professional work in Learning Engineering, Instructional Design, Learning Experience Design (LXD), Educational Technology, AI-Supported Learning, Digital Learning Innovation, and EdTech Product and Program Development. My goal is to combine learning science, human-centered design, technology, and data-informed decision-making to create effective, accessible, and learner-centered learning experiences.
